## Slide 1 — The challenge

**Build something that matters**  
Early-career developers can have career goals, learning notes, portfolio evidence and feedback spread across several tools. ImpactPath explores whether one focused workflow can make the next action and evidence of progress easier to see.

**Say:** “I chose a problem connected to my own career and to other people beginning theirs. I treated fragmentation as a hypothesis and built a way to test it.”

## Slide 3 — Idea

**ImpactPath: goal → action → evidence → feedback → reflection**

The MVP connects research records, a feedback capture form, timed task tests and an outcome dashboard. It focuses on a small repeatable career-growth loop before adding reminders or AI features.

**Say:** “The product idea is a testable workflow, not just a feature list.”
